# TrustMesh AI

### A Dynamic Economic Reputation & Business Intelligence Platform for the Informal Economy

TrustMesh AI explores a new approach to financial intelligence for the informal economy by building a dynamic **Economic Reputation Framework** that evaluates economic reliability beyond traditional credit scores.

The platform combines behavioral analytics, machine learning, business simulation, knowledge graphs, and explainable AI to understand business stability, resilience, growth potential, and financial discipline.

---

## 🚀 Project Vision

TrustMesh AI aims to build a continuously evolving economic profile for individuals and small businesses that are underserved by traditional financial systems.

Instead of asking only:

> **"Can this person repay a loan?"**

TrustMesh asks:

> **"How economically reliable is this individual or business?"**

The system brings together multiple economic signals to understand:

* Income stability
* Business consistency
* Cash-flow health
* Financial discipline
* Supplier reliability
* Growth potential
* Seasonal resilience
* Operational continuity
* Expansion readiness

---

## 💡 Core Innovations

### 1. Dynamic Economic Reputation

A multidimensional framework that evaluates economic reliability through several explainable indicators rather than reducing an individual or business to a single credit score.

### 2. Business Twin

A virtual representation of a business that can simulate possible future scenarios before decisions are made.

Examples:

* Taking a loan
* Hiring another worker
* Changing suppliers
* Increasing inventory
* Expanding the business
* Changes in operating costs

### 3. Trust Portability

A framework for combining trusted economic signals from different parts of an individual's or business's economic activity into a unified, portable Economic Reputation.

## 🔬 Research Contributions

TrustMesh AI explores five major research directions:

1. **Dynamic Economic Reputation Framework**
   A multidimensional alternative to traditional credit scoring.

2. **Business Twin Framework**
   A simulation-based approach for evaluating potential business decisions before they are made.

3. **Trust Portability Framework**
   A method for combining fragmented trust signals into a portable economic identity.

4. **Economic Knowledge Graph**
   A graph-based representation of relationships between workers, businesses, suppliers, customers, products, and economic events.

5. **Explainable Financial Intelligence**
   Transparent AI-driven insights and recommendations instead of black-box financial predictions.

## 🧠 Economic Reputation Framework

TrustMesh AI does not reduce economic reliability to a single score.

Instead, the Economic Reputation Framework evaluates multiple dimensions:

* Financial Stability
* Business Reliability
* Growth
* Risk
* Resilience
* Trust
* Expansion Readiness

These dimensions are designed to provide a more comprehensive and explainable view of economic health.

---

## 🏪 Business Twin

The Business Twin creates a virtual representation of an individual's or business's economic activity and allows users to explore potential future scenarios.

Example questions:

* What happens if I take a ₹50,000 loan?
* What happens if I hire another worker?
* What happens if I change suppliers?
* What happens if inventory increases?
* What happens if operating costs rise?
* What happens if seasonal demand decreases?

The simulator aims to estimate how these decisions could affect revenue, expenses, profitability, business risk, and repayment capability.

---

## 🔗 Trust Portability

Economic trust is often fragmented across different platforms and relationships.

For example:

* Delivery platforms may observe worker reliability.
* Suppliers may observe payment discipline.
* Customers may observe product quality.
* Landlords may observe rental payment history.

TrustMesh AI explores how these consent-based signals could contribute to a unified and portable Economic Reputation.
